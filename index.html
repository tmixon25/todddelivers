Governance

Standing up governance from zero.

The question I get asked most: how would you start a governance program when there's no framework in place? I don't start with a framework. I start with what's breaking, stand up the lightest governance that fixes it, and earn the right to scale — because governance should enable delivery, not tax it.

01

Discover

Talk to the people doing the work and the ones feeling the pain. You can't govern what you don't yet understand.

02

Scope & principles

One page: what's in, what's out, and three or four principles that define "good" — right-sized to the team's maturity.

03

Operating model & cadence

Decision rights, an intake path, and a review forum on a real rhythm. Cadence is what keeps governance alive instead of shelved.

04

Measure

Three to five KPIs, baselined — change success, outages from change, cycle time, audit findings, adoption. Evidence over opinion.

05

Report & iterate

Visible reporting, publicized wins to earn buy-in, then add rigor where it pays and cut what's just friction.

I've built this cold twice — a ServiceNow change-control and CMDB backbone at a managed-security provider, and an ITSM platform from scratch in financial services. Same playbook every time: start light, prove value, scale. The same spine covers Responsible AI governance — just swap the KPIs and the guardrails.
